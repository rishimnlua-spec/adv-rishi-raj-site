Who We Are

Advocate Rishi Raj Singh

Rishi is a dedicated and experienced legal professional enrolled at the Bar Council of Bihar, practicing at the High Court of Patna. He has built a reputation for diligence, integrity, and client-first representation.

He holds a B.A. LL.B (Hons.) degree from Maharashtra National Law University, Aurangabad, and has since devoted his career to providing expert legal counsel across a wide spectrum of legal domains including civil, criminal, constitutional, and consumer law.

Prior to practicing before the High Court of Patna, his professional experience includes practicing before the Supreme Court of India and working with a leading law firm in Mumbai.

His practice is guided by the belief that every client deserves access to skilled legal representation, regardless of the complexity of the matter.
